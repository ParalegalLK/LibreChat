## Answer

Based on the leading Sri Lankan [company law](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law) textbook by Kanaganayagam Kanag-Isvaran and Dilshani Wijayawardana, here is a comprehensive note on debentures under the Companies Act, together with the steps required to use debentures in a capital injection facility:

## 1. What is a Debenture?

- Definition: The term "debenture" has no fixed legal definition in Sri Lankan law. It generally refers to an instrument issued by a company that either creates or acknowledges a debt. It need not be under seal, and the word "debenture" need not appear on its face, provided it evidences indebtedness and an obligation to pay ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 227).
- Scope: The Companies Act defines "debenture" to include "debenture stock, bonds and any other securities of a company, whether constituting a charge on the assets of the company or not" ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 227).
- Types: Debentures may be secured, for example by a charge over company assets, or unsecured. They may be issued as individual instruments or as part of a series.

## 2. Key Features and Advantages

- Debt capital: Debentures are a means for companies to raise debt capital, often at a lower cost than equity, with interest payments typically tax-deductible ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 227).
- Control: Issuing debentures does not dilute control of the company, as debenture holders are creditors, not shareholders.
- Flexibility: Debentures can be structured with various terms regarding interest, security, redemption, and ranking.

## 3. Issuance of Debentures - Legal Requirements and Steps

### A. Authority to Issue

- Articles of Association: The company must have the power to issue debentures in its articles. If not, the articles must be amended accordingly ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 228).

### B. Board and Shareholder Approvals

- Board resolution: The board of directors must resolve to issue debentures, specifying the terms, including amount, interest, security, and redemption.
- Shareholder approval: Not generally required unless the articles or a prior resolution so provide, or if the issue constitutes a major transaction under the Act.

### C. Documentation

- Debenture instrument: Prepare the debenture certificate or instrument setting out the terms of the debt.
- Trust deed: If the debentures are secured, a trust deed is usually executed between the company and a trustee for the debenture holders ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 230).

### D. Prospectus if Public Issue

- Prospectus requirement: If debentures are offered to the public, a prospectus complying with section 37 of the Act must be issued. No application form for debentures may be issued without an accompanying prospectus ([chapter-11-allotment-of-shares.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-11-allotment-of-shares.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 129).
- Allotment timing: No allotment can be made until the third day after the prospectus is first issued ([chapter-11-allotment-of-shares.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-11-allotment-of-shares.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 129).

### E. Register and Disclosure

- Register of debenture holders: The company must maintain a register of debenture holders, open for inspection ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 229).
- Trust deed access: Debenture holders are entitled to a copy of the trust deed on request and payment of a fee ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 230).

### F. Security and Registration of Charges

- Security: If the debentures are secured, the security may be a fixed or floating charge over company assets.
- Registration: Any charge created to secure debentures must be registered with the Registrar of Companies within 21 working days of execution ([chapter-24-registration-of-charges.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-24-registration-of-charges.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 275).
- For a series of debentures, particulars of the series and a copy of the trust deed or one debenture must be filed ([chapter-24-registration-of-charges.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-24-registration-of-charges.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 278).
- Failure to register renders the security void against the liquidator and creditors, but the underlying debt remains enforceable ([chapter-24-registration-of-charges.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-24-registration-of-charges.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 279).

### G. Stamp Duty

- Stamping: Debentures are subject to stamp duty as bonds or debentures under the Stamp Ordinance, with exemptions for debentures issued under a duly stamped mortgage deed assigning property to trustees for debenture holders ([chapter-05-the-stamp-ordinance.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fthe-conveyancer-and-property-law%2Fchapter-05-the-stamp-ordinance.md&name=The%20Conveyancer%20and%20Property%20Lawyer&area=notarial), p. 157).

## 4. Redemption Rights and Section 83

- Section 83 of the Companies Act provides that a condition in a debenture or trust deed making the debenture irredeemable, or redeemable only on a remote contingency or after a long period, is not invalid. Accordingly, the company has flexibility to set redemption terms as it wishes, subject to the agreement with the debenture holders ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 231).
- Company discretion: The redemption rights, including timing, method, and contingencies, are determined by the terms set by the company in the debenture instrument or trust deed, and are not otherwise restricted by the Act.

## 5. Steps for Using Debentures in a Capital Injection Facility

1. Board approval: Pass a board resolution to issue debentures for the capital injection, specifying terms including amount, interest, security, and redemption.
2. Shareholder approval if required: Obtain shareholder approval if the transaction is a major transaction or if the articles require it.
3. Draft debenture instrument: Prepare the debenture certificate or certificates and, if secured, a trust deed.
4. Register security: If secured, register the charge with the Registrar of Companies within 21 working days.
5. Stamp duty: Ensure the debenture instrument is properly stamped.
6. Register of debenture holders: Enter details in the company's register.
7. Issue debentures: Deliver the debenture certificates to the investor or investors.
8. Prospectus if public issue: Issue a prospectus and comply with all public offering requirements.
9. Receive funds: The company receives the capital injection from the debenture subscriber or subscribers.
10. Ongoing compliance: Maintain the register, comply with trust deed obligations, and make interest and redemption payments as per the terms.

## 6. Additional Notes

- Ranking: If debentures are issued in a series, their ranking depends on the presence or absence of a pari passu clause ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 228).
- Director as trustee: A director cannot be appointed as trustee for debenture holders, except for appointments made before 2 July 1982 ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 230).
- Re-issue of redeemed debentures: The Act allows re-issue of redeemed debentures unless the articles or a resolution prohibit it ([chapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md](https://docs.desaram.ai/document.html/?view=document&path=books%2Fcompany-law%2Fchapter-21-special-provisions-as-to-debentures.md&name=Company%20Law&area=company-law), p. 231).
